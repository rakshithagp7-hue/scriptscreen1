## Adapted narration

### School children aged 10-14
1. Did you know our freshwater is limited? That means every single drop really counts!
2. First, always turn off the faucet while brushing your teeth instead of letting it run.
3. Second, help spot leaking taps and pipes. Even tiny drips waste huge amounts over time.
4. Third, take short, refreshing showers instead of filling up long baths.
5. Fourth, remind your family to run the washing machine only when it is completely full.
6. Fifth, collect rainwater outside in buckets and reuse it to give thirsty plants a drink.
7. Small habits practiced every day add up to make a giant difference for our planet!

### Senior executives
1. Clean water is finite, and meaningful stewardship starts with everyday routines at home.
2. Shut off the faucet while brushing your teeth, and repair leaking fixtures promptly. Even a minor drip wastes significant volume over time.
3. Choose brief showers over full baths to immediately reduce your daily consumption.
4. Wait for a full load before running the washing machine to make every cycle count.
5. Outside, collect rainwater in collection barrels and use it to maintain your garden and household plants.
6. Every drop matters. Small, disciplined daily habits compound into lasting change.
